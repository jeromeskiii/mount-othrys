## Greek Mythology

**Iapetus** was a Titan, son of Uranus and Gaia, and father of:
- **Atlas** (holds up heavens)
- **Prometheus** (forethought)
- **Epimetheus** (afterthought)
- **Menoetius** (mortal strength)

Iapetus represents mortality, craft, and the foundations of creation.
